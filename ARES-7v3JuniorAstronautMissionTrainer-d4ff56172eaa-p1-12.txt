  BUILDER
  A1. Working rules
      1. Build in the four phases in A4. Do not start a phase until the previous phase's
         acceptance tests pass. After each phase, report what works, what does not, and what
         you changed.

      2. Keep the simulation in a pure JavaScript module ( src/sim/ ) with no React or
          Three.js imports. The UI and 3D scene only read its output. This keeps the rules
          testable and stops 3D bugs from breaking gameplay.
      3. Never invent data. Every number or ID in src/data/nasaData.js is an object {
          value, unit, provenance, source, url, verified } . If you cannot confirm a value
          from its source, set verified:false ; the UI must then show "pending verification"
          beside it. Never fabricate flare IDs, OSDR study IDs, or quotes. Confirm that every
          URL resolves before shipping.
      4. Deterministic by seed. Use a seeded PRNG (mulberry32). The same seed produces
          the same mission.
      5. Always ship a Simple View (HUD and sliders, no 3D). It is the guaranteed-working
          path. It activates automatically if WebGL fails or the 3D scene throws (use an Error
          Boundary), and a header toggle switches to it manually.
      6. Keyboard handling. Call preventDefault on Space and arrow keys. Ignore game keys
          while a modal is open or a text field has focus. Ignore event.repeat for Q, E and
          Space. Clear all held movement keys on window blur.
      7. Accessibility. Text at least 16 px (kid UI 18 px). Touch targets at least 44 px. Visible
         focus rings. Modals trap focus and close on Esc. Respect prefers-reduced-motion
          (no shakes or pulses). No flashing more than 3 times per second. Never rely on color
          alone (see B7).
      8. No external assets. Procedural meshes only, system font stack with heavy weights,
         lucide-react icons. No audio files. Read-aloud uses window.speechSynthesis :
          feature-detect it, hide the button if unsupported, and cancel speech when a modal
          closes.
      9. Style rules. No backdrop-filter , no glassmorphism, no purple or cyan gradients, no
         generic dashboard look. Use 2px solid borders, boxy panels, and the palette: slate
           #0F172A , white #F8FAFC , Mars orange #EA580C , solar gold #EAB308 , emerald
           #10B981 , crimson #EF4444 .
   10. Never claim more than the data supports. Every on-screen number carries a
       provenance badge (B2). The text "real" appears only next to REAL NASA MEASUREMENT
          items.
   11. Performance. Cap devicePixelRatio at 1.75. Keep draw calls low (merge static rocks
          and trays, use instancing). Use at most one shadow-casting light with a 1024 map, or
          no shadows. Pause the render loop when the tab is hidden or a full-screen modal is
          open. Target 50+ fps on a mid-range tablet.

  PART B: PRODUCT SPEC

  B1. Overview
          Game: a Martian outpost trainer for ages 7–12, with an instant toggle to Judge Mode
          for older students, teachers and judges. No login, no backend.
          Mission: 7 Sols (Martian days). Each Sol, solar power is scarce. The player routes
          power (kW) to three stations. Choices persist and compound across Sols.
          Learning goal: space habitats are coupled systems, and with limited power you trade
          one risk against another.
          Challenge fit: it covers power, radiation shielding, food production and life support, so
          players feel what makes a mission succeed or fail.

  B3. Simulation engine

  B3.1 State (persists across Sols)

     Field                      Start         Meaning

      o2Water                   85            Life-support buffer (0–100), NORMALIZED GAME
                                              UNIT

      food                      80            Food stock (0–100)

      crewHealth                90            Crew readiness (0–100)

      cumRad                    0.00          Cumulative dose, mSv. Warning above 4.5, limit 6.5

      battery                   30            One-time emergency kW pool for the whole
                                              mission

      replays                   2             Replay-Sol uses left



  B3.2 Player input
  Each Sol the player sets P_shield , P_bio , P_hab in 5 kW steps (0–70 each). total =
  sum . available = solarSupply − (crewHealth < 65 at end of previous Sol ? 10 :
  0) . batteryDraw = max(0, total − available) . The move is illegal if batteryDraw >
  battery . Execute is disabled until the plan is legal, and the UI shows the battery draw live.


  B3.3 Order of operations (follow exactly)
      1. dose = 0.64 × SEP × exp(−0.032 × P_shield) ; cumRad += dose .
      2. eff = (crewHealth at start of this Sol < 65) ? 0.8 : 1.0 .
      3. foodDelta = (P_bio − 40) × 0.45 ; if foodDelta > 0 multiply by eff . food =
        clamp(food + foodDelta) .
      4. bonus = (food ≥ 65 AND P_bio ≥ 30) ? +6 : −5 (uses the updated food). Sol 4
        only: if P_bio ≥ 40 and bonus > 0 , bonus × 1.5 .

ARES-7 v3: Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer




      5. o2Water = clamp(o2Water + (P_hab − 40) × 0.55 + bonus) .
      6. radPenalty = dose > 1.10 ? (dose − 1.10) × 8.5 : 0 . scarcity = (o2Water <
        55 ? 7 : 0) + (food < 55 ? 6 : 0) (uses updated values).
      7. crewHealth = clamp(crewHealth + (P_hab − 40) × 0.45 − radPenalty −
        scarcity) .
      8. battery −= batteryDraw .
      9. Return a structured trace array of every step (inputs, formula, delta, status). The
         Why-Chain renders only from this trace.

   clamp is 0–100.


  B3.4 Scarcity note
  On a quiet Sol, break-even is about 40 (bio) + 40 (hab) + shield. "Comfort level" is 45 kW
  per station, so 135 kW total. Supply of 65–100 kW means players can never run everything
  comfortably. They must choose which risk to carry.

  B4. Status labels and outcomes
          Stocks ( o2Water , food , crewHealth ): NOMINAL at 60 or above; STRAINED 40–59;
          CRITICAL below 40.
          Daily dose: NOMINAL ≤ 0.70; STRAINED ≤ 1.10; CRITICAL above 1.10.
          Cumulative dose: NOMINAL ≤ 4.5; STRAINED ≤ 6.5; CRITICAL above 6.5.

                 ★★★ "Flight Director": all three stocks ≥ 65 and cumRad ≤ 5.5.
                 ★★ "Mission Specialist": all three ≥ 50 and cumRad ≤ 6.5.
                 ★ "Cadet": the mission finishes with lessons learned.

  B5. Seeded Randomizer
  A seed field (editable, shareable) drives a PRNG. Per Sol, draw supply from {100, 95, 90, 75,
  65} and an event from the flare catalog. Guarantee at least one dust Sol (supply ≤ 75) and
  one flare Sol (SEP ≥ 3.8). Site B weights dust more. Show the seed on the report so
  teachers can assign the same mission.


  B6. 3D scene and controls
          Camera: orthographic isometric (for example position [9,9,9], looking at the origin),
          fixed.
          Terrain: low-poly displaced plane (seeded noise), rock clusters, Mars-orange palette.
          Pad radius about 7.
          Astronaut: procedural capsule body, helmet sphere, floating direction cone. Start
           (0,0,0) . Speed about 4 units/s. Clamp delta to avoid jumps after tab switches.
          Circle collision with stations (radius about 1.4).
          Stations (proximity ring radius about 2.2; highlight label when in range):
                 North [0,0,−4.2] : Shield & Comms Relay. Wireframe dome; opacity = 0.15 + 0.6
                 × (P/60).
                 West [−4.2,0,2.2] : CSA Bio-Dome. Geodesic dome and crop trays; trays glow
                 with P_bio ; dim and brown when food < 55 .
                 East [4.2,0,2.2] : Habitat & ECLSS. Cylinder with lit windows (count = round(6
                 × P/60)) and a rotating radiator; rotation scales with P_hab .

                 WASD / Arrows: move.
